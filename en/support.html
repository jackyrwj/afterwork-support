Common questions

  • Notifications are not showing up. Allow them in Settings → Notifications → AfterWork.
  • How do I change my clock-out or focus time? Open the You tab and tap the time under Times.
  • How do I change a task? Open Plan, tap the task, and edit its name, icon, color, and weekly schedule.
  • How do I change which animal shows up? Open You → Companions, tap a frame, and pick the state and background color.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open the paywall from You → Unlock everything and tap Restore purchases.
  • How do I delete my data? Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.
