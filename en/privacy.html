AfterWork Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

AfterWork takes your privacy seriously. The app has no accounts to create, no ads, and no third-party analytics.

What we store

Your work days, times, tasks, focus sessions, and personalization choices are stored locally on your device. The developer does not upload any of it to a server.

Notifications

With your permission, the app sends local notifications to tell you when a focus session starts, finishes, or is still running. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details. To confirm whether Premium is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase validation service. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (products, transaction dates, and receipts) together with a random anonymous ID generated by the app. This information is not linked to your name, email, or Apple Account, and is used only to deliver and restore your purchases. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Sharing

The app does not sell or rent your data, and does not use it for advertising or tracking. Apart from the purchase information described above, which is shared with RevenueCat only to validate purchases, nothing leaves your device.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data it stored on your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
